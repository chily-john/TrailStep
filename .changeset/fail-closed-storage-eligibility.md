Automatic storage retention is now fail-closed. `planStorageLifecycle`, `applyStorageLifecycle` and direct `archiveRun` authorize a hot-run archive/delete only from a new `assessStorageEligibility` result: a complete strict read of `events.jsonl` containing a current-cycle `workflow.completed`/`workflow.failed`/`workflow.cancelled` event, a present and readable `track.json`, no pin, and ownership that is strictly observed as absent (missing lock or an assessable dead pid). Eventless terminal tracks are preserved rather than treated as terminal proof (D-LOCK-2), and a terminal event whose track is missing or unreadable is preserved for the D6 recovery protocol. Malformed, partial or unreadable event trails, unreadable or malformed tracks, and malformed, unreadable or unassessable locks produce no automatic action; direct archive refuses them with a diagnostic. Explicit `deleteRun` remains an operator action for non-terminal runs but now also refuses live, malformed, unreadable or unassessable lock ownership. The new `observeRunLock` seam keeps missing, malformed, unreadable, live, dead and unknown ownership distinct without widening the tolerant read-only `isRunLockLive`. Eligibility is still revalidated at the action boundary only as far as D4 can; the archive/delete race is not solved and remains D5 work. `trailstep storage gc` may therefore retain more legacy or inconsistent runs, and its usage text now says so.
